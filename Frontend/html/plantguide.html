UNDERSTAND LIGHT

Choose the Right Light

Different plants need different amounts of sunlight.

Bright Direct Light

Strong sunlight that reaches the plant directly.

Example: Aloe Vera

Bright Indirect Light

Bright surroundings without direct harsh sunlight.

Example: Peace Lily

Medium Light

A place with moderate natural light.

Example: Money Plant

Low Light

Areas with little natural sunlight.

Example: Snake Plant

WATERING GUIDE

Water Your Plants the Right Way

Avoid watering your plants on a fixed schedule. Instead, check the soil before watering.

01

Check if the top soil feels dry.

02

Water slowly until excess water drains out.

03

Never allow the plant to sit in water.

04

Reduce watering during cooler months.

ESSENTIAL CARE

Give Your Plants the Care They Need

Sunlight

Place your plant where it receives the right amount of natural light.

Water

Water according to the plant's needs and soil moisture.

Temperature

Keep plants away from extreme heat and cold.

Humidity

Tropical plants usually enjoy higher humidity levels.

Fertilizer

Feed plants occasionally during their growing season.

Pruning

Remove dead and damaged leaves to encourage healthy growth.
